# Message Templates

Fill-in-the-blank templates keyed to the [runbook](runbook.md) triggers. Personalize the bracketed fields and the first sentence; keep the structure. Goal: every message is a 2-minute task.

Conventions: `[T]` = current lease end date, `[RATE]` = current monthly rate, `[UNIT]` = Fremont/Newark listing name.

---

## P1 · Plans check-in (T−75)

> Hi [NAME] — hope the place is treating you well! Quick check-in: is there anything in the unit that needs attention?
>
> Also, no pressure at all yet, but as we get a couple of months out from [T], I'll start planning the calendar. If you already have a sense of whether you'll be extending your contract or moving on, I'd love a heads-up — extending is easy on my end if you want the option.

## E1 · Extension offer (T−60)

> Hi [NAME] — your lease currently runs through [T]. You've been a great tenant and I'd be glad to have you stay longer.
>
> If you extend through **[ENGINEERED END DATE]** ([X] more months, prorated exactly to your contract if needed), I'll keep your rate at **[RATE]/month** — no increase. *(If applicable: …and take [3–5]% off for the extension period.)*
>
> If you'd rather go month-to-month after [T], I can do that at [RATE × 1.08–1.10]/month with 30 days' written notice.
>
> Could you let me know by **[T−45 DATE]**? After that I need to open the dates to new bookings, and I'd rather give you first claim on them.

Pricing rule: default is current rate flat; discount only when the extension moves a Red end date to Green (design §7 caps what a save is worth).

## C1 · Engineered end-date counter (at inquiry/signing)

> Those dates could work — one adjustment on my side: rather than ending [THEIR PROPOSED END], I can do **[ENGINEERED END DATE]**, with the extra [N] days simply prorated at the monthly rate (no premium for the odd length). That's [X] months + [N] days total.
>
> If your contract dates are fixed and that doesn't work, tell me — we can likely still figure something out.

Use when their end date is Yellow/Red (design §4). Prefer lengthening the stay. If they can't flex, accept and apply the next term-premium tier.

## D1 · Departure confirmation (T−45)

> Hi [NAME] — thanks for letting me know. Confirming in writing: your lease ends **[T]** and you'll be moving out by then; your deposit will be handled per the lease after move-out inspection. I'll send move-out logistics closer to the date.
>
> Since we're confirmed, I'll begin advertising the unit for dates after yours. If your plans change and you'd like to extend after all, tell me right away — once a new booking is signed the dates are gone.

## I1 · Furnished Finder inquiry reply (same day)

> Hi [NAME], thanks for reaching out about [UNIT]! It's available from **[AVAILABLE DATE]**. Quick questions so I can confirm fit:
>
> 1. What are your exact dates — start and end? (If they're tied to a contract, we can prorate to match it precisely.)
> 2. Who would be staying?
> 3. Is your contract/assignment confirmed?
>
> The monthly rate for a [X]-month stay is **[RATE PER TERM TABLE]**, utilities [included/handled how], no booking fees — we lease directly.
>
> *(Unreviewed unit only:)* This is the newer of our two units — same owner and same standard as our [OTHER CITY] unit, which has [N] reviews you can check here: [LINK].

## I2 · Airbnb inquiry reply (within hours — protects response metrics)

> Hi [NAME], thanks for the inquiry — the place is available for your dates. It's set up specifically for traveling professionals ([fast Wi-Fi / dedicated workspace / full kitchen / parking]). A couple of quick questions: what brings you to the area, and are your dates fixed or flexible on the back end?
>
> *(If their end date is Yellow/Red and stay ≥ 2 months, fold in C1's adjustment.)*
> *(Unreviewed unit only:)* This listing is newer, but we're experienced hosts — our other Bay Area unit's reviews are at [LINK/mention].

Never let an Airbnb inquiry or request expire — decline with a friendly note if it's a clear no.

## F2 · No-reply bump #1 (+48h after first touch)

> Hi [NAME] — following up on [UNIT] for [THEIR START DATE]. Still looking? These dates tend to go quickly and I'd hate for you to miss it — once your contract's confirmed I can hold the dates 48 hours while you finalize. Happy to answer anything or do a quick video tour.

## F3 · No-reply bump #2 (+6 days — final unsolicited touch)

> Hi [NAME] — last note from me so I'm not cluttering your inbox! Two things that might help if you're still deciding:
>
> 1. You can see what staying with us is like from our [OTHER/SAME UNIT] reviews: [LINK].
> 2. If your contract dates don't line up perfectly with what's listed, we prorate to the exact day — start and end whenever your assignment does.
>
> If the timing's just off for this assignment, tell me your next contract window and I'll reach out when we have matching dates. Good luck with the move either way!

## A1 · Alternative offer (requested dates/unit unavailable)

> Hi [NAME], thanks for reaching out! Our [REQUESTED UNIT] is booked for those dates — but our [OTHER CITY] unit is available from [DATE] and is the same setup and standard ([cross-reference reviews if offering the newer unit]). Details: [1–2 differentiators — commute anchors, parking].
>
> *(Variant — nothing fits:)* We're fully booked for that window. If you'd like, I'll keep your dates on file and reach out the moment something matching opens — and if your next contract brings you back to the Bay Area, we'd love to host you then.
>
> *(Variant — sub-30-day request:)* We're set up for stays of 30 days or longer (local regulations). If your assignment could work with a 30-day minimum — even with your actual occupancy a bit shorter — happy to talk through it.

## F1 · Stalled-lead re-contact (T−30)

> Hi [NAME] — you asked about [UNIT] a few weeks back. The [DATE] opening is still available and I'm now confirming the schedule for that window. Still interested? Happy to hold it for 48 hours if you're ready to move forward.

## M1 · Move-out logistics (T−7)

> Hi [NAME] — ahead of your move-out on [T]: please plan to be out by [TIME], leave keys [WHERE/HOW], and leave the unit broom-clean (we handle the deep clean). I'll do the inspection that day and your deposit will be returned per the lease within [N] days. Thanks for being a great tenant — happy to be a rental reference anytime.

## Screening note (all channels, before signing)

Qualify every serious lead on: confirmed contract/offer letter (travel nurses: agency + facility; corporate: employer letter), exact dates, occupants, and budget acknowledged. For FF direct leases, run the standard application/screening before signing — platform bookings rely on Airbnb's verification instead.
